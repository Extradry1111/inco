Built on Inco Lightning, not vibes

Standard onchain games can't hide anything — every mine position is readable the moment it's written. Inco Lightning adds a confidentiality layer to Base, so state can be encrypted, computed on, and only revealed when your game logic says so.

01

Encrypted grid

Every tile is stored as an ebool — an encrypted true/false. Nobody, including us, can read the board before it's revealed tile by tile.

02

Onchain randomness

Mine placement is drawn with Inco's TEE-based randomness at round start — no oracle, no seed anyone could front-run.

03

Attested reveal

Revealing a tile isn't a guess — it's a signed decryption attestation from Inco's covalidator network, verified onchain before the game trusts it.

// the loop

How a round actually works

Three onchain steps. No backend, no house edge you can't audit.

01

Stake & encrypt

Pick a stake and a mine count. The contract draws 25 encrypted values on Base — your board exists, fully sealed.

02

Reveal, tile by tile

Each click requests a decryption, waits for Inco's signed attestation, then submits it onchain. Safe tiles push your multiplier up.

03

Cash out or push your luck

Walk away anytime with your current multiplier, or keep revealing. Hit a mine and the stake feeds straight into the Megapot jackpot.
